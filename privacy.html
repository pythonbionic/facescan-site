FaceScan Privacy Policy

Effective: November 15, 2026 · PYTHON OPTIC INC ("we", "us")

FaceScan measures faces. We designed it so that we never receive your face.

What FaceScan does

FaceScan uses your iPhone's TrueDepth camera to capture the three-dimensional shape of your face, measure facial landmarks and pupillary distance (PD), and produce an export file (meshes and measurements) for your own use in measurement workflows.

Your face data stays on your phone

All capture and processing happens on your device. FaceScan has no servers and no accounts. We do not receive, store, or have access to your scans.

The app keeps a record of your consent — the date and the policy version you agreed to — on this device only.

FaceScan stores no photographs. Camera frames are used live and in memory only.

A scan exists only in the app's temporary storage on your device until you export or delete it.

The only way scan data leaves your device is if you choose Share in the export screen and send the file somewhere. That destination is your choice; whoever you send it to handles it under their own terms.

We never sell face data. We never use face data — or anything derived from it — for advertising, marketing, data mining, identification, or building a profile of you. FaceScan contains no advertising or analytics software and no third-party trackers.

Biometric data notice

A scan of face geometry is treated as biometric data under laws such as the Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act. FaceScan collects that geometry only after you agree to the in-app notice, only for the purpose of producing your measurements and export file, and only on your device. Our retention schedule: scan data is destroyed when you delete it in the app, when you delete the app, or when iOS clears the app's temporary storage — in every case far within the three-year outer limit those laws set. Because we never receive your scans, there is nothing on our side to retain, disclose, or sell.

What the export file contains

The ZIP you may choose to share contains: face meshes (PLY/OBJ), depth maps, landmark and measurement data (JSON), and a session record including your device model, iOS version, and capture timestamps. It contains no photographs and no name or contact details.

Children

FaceScan is a measurement tool intended for adults and professionals. It is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no data from anyone — including children — on our side.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the in-app notice before the change applies to new scans. Questions: pythonbioactive@gmail.com
