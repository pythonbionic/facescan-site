FaceScan Support

What FaceScan does

FaceScan measures your pupillary distance (PD) — total and per eye — for ordering glasses online. It uses the TrueDepth depth camera in your iPhone to measure in real 3D, then keeps your scan and measurements as one export file that belongs to you.

Requirements

An iPhone with Face ID (a TrueDepth front camera) running iOS 16 or later. On a device without a TrueDepth camera, the app explains this and cannot measure.

How to scan

Agree to the consent notice, allow camera access, and tap "Begin scan". Put the ball in each circle and hold it there — each pose captures itself. Then do the same for the three depth stills. Your PD is on the completion screen, and "Share export file" gives you the full scan as one ZIP.

Your data

Everything is measured and stored on your iPhone. There is no account and no upload. Delete all scan data any time in Settings, or by deleting the app. See the privacy policy.
